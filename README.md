## 🎯 Business Problem

A sales company has executives working across 8 cities: Chennai, Delhi, Mumbai, Nagpur, Patna, Pune, Ranchi and Surat. The sales manager needs quick answers to these questions:

- Who are the top and bottom performers by total sales?
- Which executives are hitting their sales targets, and by how much?
- Which executives are falling behind, and how far are they from target?
- How does performance change from city to city?

Checking this manually across many sheets is slow. This dashboard gives the answers in one click.

---

## 📈 Key Metrics (KPIs)

| KPI | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Total Sales (descending) | Top-performing sales executives |
| Total Sales (ascending) | Lowest-performing sales executives |
| Target Hit % | How much of the target each executive achieved |
| Away From Target % | How far each executive is from the target |

## ⚙️ Key Features

- ✅ Sales analysis by sales executive
- ✅ Target achievement percentage analysis
- ✅ Performance against sales targets
- ✅ **City slicer** for dynamic filtering
- ✅ **VBA macros** that connect one slicer to all four PivotTables, so there is no need to set Report Connections for each table manually
- ✅ Pivot tables for summarising data
- ✅ Interactive charts and visualisations

## 🔍 Insights

*(Based on the dashboard view with the **Delhi** city filter selected.)*

- **Ani kuttan** is the top seller with **382** sales and also has the highest target hit rate (**76.4%**).
- **Ritu Bhatnagar** is a strong second on total sales (**371**) and has a **74.2%** target hit rate.
- **Bhagwat Singh** and **Manju Tiwari Lab** have the lowest target hit rates among the top group (**65.4%** and **63.6%**), even though they sit in the top 5 by sales.
- At the other end, **Jyoti Tulsani** (228) and **Mubeen Khan** (209) are among the lowest by total sales.
- **Praveen Kumar** is the furthest from target at **66.8%**, followed by **Mubeen Khan** at **58.2%**.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

1. **Coach the bottom performers.** Pair executives such as Praveen Kumar and Mubeen Khan with top sellers like Anikuttan for guidance.
2. **Review targets for high-volume executives with low target hit.** Check whether the targets are set too high.
3. **Recognise and reward top performers** to keep them motivated.
4. **Compare cities.** Use the slicer to find which cities consistently under-perform and focus support there.
